## Status legend and interpretation

- **PASS** — the acceptance check is supported by the cited evidence **within its stated scope**. A code/test or static-inventory pass is not a claim of browser, database, device, production, or platform-wide acceptance unless that evidence is specifically present.
- **FAIL** — an observed, direct mismatch or missing required product capability exists in the audited baseline.
- **PARTIAL** — separately stated acceptance subcriteria are positively verified, but the row is not fully accepted and the remaining scope is not otherwise blocked. Partial implementation evidence is described in row notes; it is not counted as completion.
- **BLOCKED** — the requirement is not accepted because material evidence is unavailable, scope is incomplete, or an owner/authorization decision is required. This takes precedence when full acceptance still depends on an unresolved test or decision.
- **NOT TESTED** — a conditional or tool-dependent path was not invoked, or no relevant creative deliverable was requested. This does not imply that the product supports it.
- **NOT APPLICABLE** — evidence establishes that a requirement does not apply to the audited product/scope; conditional or uninvoked work is not enough to use this status.

Evidence IDs are defined in `VERIFICATION_EVIDENCE.md`. A status never transfers from current `main` to this local worktree, from an open PR proposal to `main`, or from a mock test to a real database.
