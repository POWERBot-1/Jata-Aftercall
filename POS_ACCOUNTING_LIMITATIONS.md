# POS accounting: current behavior and acceptance gaps

This describes the implementation, **not** an approved complete accounting policy. No report should be treated as a complete period financial statement until its sources and reconciliation have been implemented and verified together.

## Current sources and dates

- The sales and tender-breakdown reports select sales by their **sale creation time** and show their current refund/void state. A later correction can change an earlier sale-cohort report. It is not an as-of-period-end reconstruction or a period cash-flow report.
- The refund report selects settled POS `OUT` / `REFUND` payment rows by their **payment creation time**, including payouts on earlier sales. Split tenders generate separate rows. It excludes credit reversals, goods returned, and Payment Wallet refunds; it is marked partial. More than 5,000 matching rows refuses the report rather than presenting a truncated subtotal.
- The POS refund path limits cash and other payouts to the corresponding settled original tenders, less previous payouts; credit reversal reduces the outstanding receivable, not the cash drawer. Named returned items create stock `RETURN` movements, with per-line quantity guarded against duplicate returns. Wallet-settled sales use the separate wallet refund flow.
- Provider wallet refunds can be asynchronous; a request is not proof of settlement. Provider completion and duplicate/mismatch handling belong to the wallet ledger. The POS refund payout report does not incorporate this ledger.
- The drawer calculation uses settled POS cash payment movements by payment creation time, not the original sale's date; it is a net movement for that day, not a verified physical count or a carry-forward opening balance. Expenses are separately recorded and must not be subtracted again from the payment ledger.
- Immutable finalized captures record a whole-business completed UTC date range, freezing the sale cohort's state at finalization and the period's POS ledger, credit entries, audit events, expenses and inventory movements. A capture is **not** necessarily the balance at period end. New captures include customer/supplier credit party identities and payment links, plus additional payment, expense and inventory references. Earlier immutable captures are not rewritten and may lack these fields; these references alone do not provide period reconciliation. New captures also show a clearly limited opening/movement/closing projection for each customer and supplier based on recorded credit entries before and during the period. This projection is unavailable if prior history exceeds 5,000 entries or contains invalid amounts; it does not verify ledger completeness, stored balances, or other financial sources. Corrections produce separate superseding revisions, which must not be added to previous revisions. Branch-scoped finalization is unsupported. Captures refuse inputs beyond their bounded limit rather than persist incomplete data.

## Limits and unresolved work

- Sale, expense, payment, inventory movement and order inputs used by reports and day closing are capped at 5,000. The report endpoint conservatively refuses when any input reaches the cap (including exactly 5,000), rather than emitting incomplete totals. Credit-age reports refuse if the complete credit-entry read exceeds 5,000 rows. A shorter period can help period-based queries; daily closing and tenant-wide credit ageing may require operational support. The dashboard hides the affected sales, order or expense cards when their source reaches the cap, without hiding unrelated cards; the credit screen refuses to render figures if its complete credit-entry read exceeds 5,000 rows. Other bounded reads outside these paths require separate review.
- The expense screen's recorded total and category subtotals use a tenant/branch/time-scoped database aggregation rather than the former 500-row sample. Its detail list is capped at 200 and discloses when it may be limited; the expense API exposes `rowsMayBeLimited`. This is a recorded expense total, not a complete financial statement.
- The sales API's top-item ranking is unavailable, rather than based on a silent sample, when more than 2,000 sales match. Below that cap it uses original sale-item quantities, including voids and returns; it is not net retained volume.
- The report's sales detail shows at most 200 non-voided rows; the totals are computed from the full fetched cohort below the cap. Customer and supplier statements refuse when more than 200 ledger entries exist or when the complete ledger does not reconcile to the stored account balance. Customer detail's gross and count describe at most 50 recent sales, not lifetime totals. Credit decisions use a FIFO ageing convention: credits reduce the oldest debits first, and the complete ledger must reconcile to the stored balance; a mismatched or over-5,000-entry history refuses new credit. With an outstanding balance, sale-linked reversals also cause refusal because invoice-specific allocation is unavailable. The credit screen and credit reports also allocate open debit amounts across age buckets under this convention and refuse unreconciled histories, sale-linked reversals with a balance, or more than 5,000 ledger entries across the business and party type. It is not an invoice-specific allocation; reports show current balances rather than period-end balances, and an account row’s age describes its oldest open lot, not the age of the whole balance.
- Profitability combines a mutable sale cohort with expenses in their occurrence period; it is always marked partial, and missing costs or unallocated refund costs make its numerical estimate less reliable.
- A complete authoritative period statement needs a consistent event/settlement model covering credit, returns, wallet/provider settlement, adjustments and each supported tender, plus tests for actual cross-period operations, concurrency and limits. Historical mutable sale fields must not be presented as immutable period facts.
- The actual UTC-midnight scenario remains unexecuted. A short elapsed boundary or backdated test is not equivalent.

The offline `npm run release:acceptance` inventory explicitly marks authoritative financial
reconciliation incomplete. It cannot establish database, provider or cross-period accounting
correctness and is not a substitute for the required independent PostgreSQL acceptance suite.
